Flexi Day is a shared calendar for time off and attendance. People belong to an organization,
which holds one or more groups. Each group has its own working days, holiday country, members and
approvers.

## What people do in Flexi Day

- **Time off.** Members send requests for vacation and other absences on the {{ui:nav.requests}}
  page. Approvers in the group approve or reject them. Quotas track how many days each person has
  in a year.
- **The calendar.** The {{ui:nav.dashboard}} shows who is away. The {{ui:nav.report}} page sums up
  time off for a group or a person, with an Excel export.
- **Calendar sync.** Time off can appear in Google or Microsoft calendars through a feed or a
  direct sync.
- **Attendance.** Where the organization turns it on, people clock in and out, and group admins
  and organization administrators see worked time on {{ui:nav.teamAttendance}}.
- **Directory.** On Enterprise, the people directory keeps employments and profiles.
- **Group events and birthdays** show on the calendar beside time off.

## Roles

The owner pays for the organization and can name org admins. Each group has a manager, approvers
and members. What a person can see and change depends on these roles.

## Plans

Organizations start on the Free plan. Pro costs {{price:PRO.monthly}} a month and Enterprise
{{price:ENTERPRISE.monthly}} a month, excluding VAT. The owner manages the plan on the
{{ui:nav.billing}} page.

## AI features

The Assistant is a chat in the web app on Enterprise. AI connections let apps such as Claude or
ChatGPT work with Flexi Day on Pro and Enterprise. The owner turns each of them on.
