My attendance, self-service

What an employee sees once their organization lets them enter and correct their own attendance. The Today tab becomes Day and steps back like Week and Month, so a past day can be opened, corrected or filled. "Add session" appears only on days inside the window. Outside it, or with self-service off, the day stays readable and says who to ask instead of offering a button that would be refused.
