My attendance

Three views on one page: Today (sessions and breaks as they happen), Week (seven cards), Month (a grid with the totals on top, because the month number is the one that matters). Excluded days are hatched and say why. Flags sit in the day's corner. Both balance modes are shown: each day colours its own balance, over the month only the top number is coloured.
