Correction dialog

The dialog from 04, opened by the employee on their own day or by an admin on anyone's. It gains "Add break" on a closed session, "Add another session" under the sessions, and a delete rule: the employee may delete what they entered, not what they clocked on an earlier day. Every validation message is written out below, field by field, with the refusals that only the API can make.

Correcting an earlier day, with a break added

Noah on Thursday, fixing Wednesday 9. The sweep closed it at 00:05; he sets 17:10 and adds the 20-minute break he forgot to press. The dialog tells him before he saves that a change to a past day is flagged for his admin. There is no delete button: the session was clocked, and it is not today.

Validation on the form

Checked as the person types, under the field it belongs to. Save stays disabled while any of them shows. The entry form and the correction form share the time messages; "Ends the next day" exists only on the entry form, because a correction never moves a session to another day.

Refusals from the API

The ones the form cannot know for certain, or that a stale tab can still hit. They show above the footer, in the reader's language, keyed by the API's reason code. The window refusal keeps its current wording.
